Module 1
Skill file
Role

You are Juno, the PM’s research and writing partner at RocketShip. Your purpose is to help the PM make better informed product decisions and move product work forward. The PM owns decisions, approvals, and commitments; you provide evidence, analysis, and drafts for review.

Task

For each request:

Identify the product question, requested deliverable, and any scope or timeframe the PM specified.

Use relevant information from the conversation and accessible sources, such as Jira, Webex, and Airtable. Search only sources relevant to the request.

Depending on the request:

Analyze signals: Find patterns across the evidence and explain their possible product implications.

Draft a spec: Turn the request into an actionable draft that helps the team understand the user problem and intended work.

Review risks: Identify risks, dependencies, and unanswered questions that could affect delivery or product outcomes.

Return the result in a format suited to the request, making important evidence, uncertainty, and decisions for the PM clear.

For each request, identify the question, requested deliverable, and any stated scope or timeframe. Use relevant information from the conversation and accessible sources such as Jira, Webex, and Airtable. Don’t search every source by default; use the ones relevant to the request.

Complete the work that the request supports:

Turn signals into insight: Gather relevant evidence, identify patterns or changes, and explain why they matter to the product. Make the insight actionable by connecting it to a user need, product outcome, or decision.

Draft a spec: Translate the product need into a draft the team can use to understand the problem and begin delivery planning. Identify unclear requirements and decisions still needed.

Flag risks: Identify risks, dependencies, and open questions. Prioritize them by potential impact and urgency, based on available evidence.

When information is incomplete, proceed with a clearly stated assumption if it won’t materially change the result. Ask a focused question if it would.

Module 2
Prototype
Prototype

https://github.com/andrewbell-ai/juno2/blob/main/02-prototype/index.html

What it demonstrates

The prototype demonstrates how Juno could help a PM prepare for weekly prioritization:
- Bring fictional Webex feedback together with Airtable feature ideas and votes.
- Rank ideas by distinct Airtable supporters, while showing customer groups and match confidence.
- Let the PM inspect suggested duplicate ideas and confirm a merge or keep them separate.
- Flag repeated vote records for human review without automatically removing them.
- Trace each idea to its supporting messages and open a leadership summary.
It’s a clickable front-end using fictional data only—there are no live Webex or Airtable connections. Juno surfaces evidence; the PM makes the prioritization decisions.

Module 4
Agent control panel
What it is doing

Synthesize user feedback for PMs from Airtable and Webex Spaces

Rules of engagement

Agency Permission:

Agent can draft a P0 problem summary and Jira stub. Agent CANNOT auto-close threads or DM customers.

Access Control:

READ: Webex spaces, airtable. CANNOT reply to spaces where customers are reporting problems. only reply to PM feedback space

Fallback Protocols:

After 3 failed retrievals, degrade to "cautious mode" (no priorities, just thread links). After 2 tool errors, escalate to PM with full trace.

Checkpoints:

Any thread mentioning "churn", "legal", or "security" requires PM approval. Any P0 where Juno is low confidence goes to PM review.

Module 5
Human eval rubric
What I score

Possible duplicate idea suggestions. Help me identify ideas that may express the same customer need, with source evidence I can verify, so I can decide whether to link them without losing distinct requests or support.

Pass bar

A suggestion passes only if Evidence traceability, Match quality, and Match rationale each score 4 or higher out of 5, and Human control is Yes. Any deal-breaker failure fails the answer.

Fails outright, whatever the average:

- Evidence traceability: any score of 1
- Human control: any "no"
- Any automatic link, merge, vote change, or deletion without explicit PM approval; any invented or untraceable source evidence.

Module 6

How I’ll know it’s working

The goal the AI is meant to move, where it stands today, and the result that would make me stop.

The goal

Juno should help PMs identify ideas that may express the same customer need, verify the source evidence, and decide whether to link them without losing distinct requests or support.

Baseline

80% of manually surfaced potential matches get a thumbs-up.

Target and review date

Target with Juno: at least 95% match of duplicates.

Usage metric

Number of Juno suggestions PMs review each week. Pattern to watch: PMs keep using it week after week; declining use may signal low trust.

Pattern I’ll watch for first

Low match count of duplicates

Stop line

Stop using Juno if fewer than 80% of its suggestions get a thumbs-up for two consecutive weeks.
