# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Possible duplicate idea suggestions
- **Value proposition:** Help me identify ideas that may express the same customer need, with source evidence I can verify, so I can decide whether to link them without losing distinct requests or support.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence traceability (deal-breaker) | Can the reviewer open source evidence for both ideas and verify that it supports the suggested match? | 1 to 5 | One or both ideas have no source link, the link is broken, or the cited evidence does not support the proposed match. | Both ideas have source links, but one citation is incomplete or only weakly supports the match. | Both ideas link to clear, relevant source evidence, and the evidence supports the stated reason they may be duplicates. |
| Match quality | Do the two ideas describe the same underlying customer need, rather than merely sharing similar wording? | 1 to 5 | The ideas ask for clearly different outcomes or workflows; overlap is only superficial wording. | The ideas are related, but the evidence shows meaningful differences or leaves it unclear whether they can be linked. | The ideas express the same core customer need and desired outcome; differences are mainly phrasing or detail, supported by the evidence. |
| Match rationale | Does Juno explain why these ideas may be duplicates using evidence from both sides? | 1 to 5 | No useful rationale, an invented explanation, or a claim unsupported by either source. | A plausible explanation is given, but it is generic or does not clearly distinguish the shared need from the differences. | A concise explanation identifies the shared customer need, notes meaningful differences or uncertainty, and points to supporting evidence from both ideas. |
| Human control (deal-breaker) | Does Juno only suggest possible duplicates and leave linking, merging, and vote changes to the PM? | Yes / No | No: Juno links or merges ideas, changes votes, or removes source records without the PM's explicit approval. | n/a | Yes: Juno only proposes a match; the PM makes the link/merge decision, and votes or source ideas remain unchanged unless approved. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Score 10 suggestions each week, or all if fewer than 10. Include a random sample plus low-confidence suggestions and cases the PM found unclear.
- **Who scores them with you:** The PM owner scores the weekly sample; one PM peer independently scores the same sample once a month.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If scores differ by 2 or more points, or reviewers disagree on Human control, compare the source records and agree on a label. Record unresolved cases for PM adjudication, then clarify the rubric wording before the next review.

## Pass bar

A suggestion passes only if Evidence traceability, Match quality, and Match rationale each score 4 or higher out of 5, and Human control is Yes. Any deal-breaker failure fails the answer.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Evidence traceability: any score of 1
- Human control: any "no"
- Any automatic link, merge, vote change, or deletion without explicit PM approval; any invented or untraceable source evidence.
